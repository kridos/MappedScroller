# MappedScroller privacy policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

MappedScroller does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data or browsing data.

**What it reads, and why.** When you link two tabs, the extension reads the headings, question numbers and
scroll position of the pages in those two tabs, so it can line them up. This happens only in your browser,
only for the two tabs you link, and only while they are linked.

**Where that information goes.** Nowhere outside your browser. Headings and scroll positions are passed
between the two linked tabs inside your browser. They are kept in the browser's temporary session storage,
which is cleared when the browser closes. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server.
The extension makes no network requests and has no analytics or tracking.

**Remote code.** None. All code is included in the extension package.

**Contact.** For questions about this policy, open an issue on the extension's support page.
